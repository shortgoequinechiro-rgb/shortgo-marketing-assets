## Geographic priority (HARD RULE)

Every post must be written for — and aim to attract — clients in **Texas**
or **Montana**. If a post could be about any horse anywhere, rewrite it so
a TX or MT rider sees themselves in it.

Concrete ways to do this:
- Drop TX / MT place names in captions ("DFW," "North Texas," "Coppell,"
  "Fort Worth," "Great Falls," "Havre").
- Reference TX / MT events ("before the Fort Worth Stock Show," "heading to
  Great Falls fair week").
- Show local barns, arenas, landscapes — not generic stock imagery.
- In CTAs, name the town/date ("we're in Frisco Saturday — DM to book").

Out-of-area followers are fine, but content that doesn't pull a TX or MT
owner toward booking is wasted budget. When in doubt, tilt TX — that's
where volume is. Montana content runs during the Montana season.

## Target audience

**Primary:** performance horse owners and trainers who compete in **Texas or
Montana**.
- Barrel racers (WPRA, NBHA, jackpot circuit)
- Ropers (team ropers, breakaway, tie-down)
- Cutters (NCHA)
- Ranch / ranch riding
- Reiners, rodeo athletes generally

**Secondary:** sport horse / hunter-jumper crowd in DFW (lower priority but
overlaps geography).

**Tertiary:** pleasure / trail riders who notice their horse moving stiff.
Don't lead with this segment — but don't exclude them either.

**Voice the audience speaks in:** plain, practical, results-focused. They
talk about their horse's "short go," "clicking lead," "stopping hard," being
"off behind." They trust referrals from other riders and vets more than any
logo or credential.

## High-value messaging pillars

In priority order:
1. **Results on performance.** Times drop, stops improve, lead changes clean
   up, stride evens out. Show this with before/after clips whenever possible.
2. **Pain relief you can see.** Visible changes in gait, posture, attitude,
   willingness to collect. "Horse came in crooked, left square."
3. **Mobile = we meet you where you're at.** No trailering a sore horse. We
   come to the barn the morning of your run.
4. **cAVCA-certified.** Dr. Leo is certified by the American Veterinary
   Chiropractic Association — this matters to the buyer who reads carefully.
   Don't lead with it; back up claims with it.
5. **Real rider, real rigs.** Short Go is a rodeo-native operation, not a
   small-animal vet's side hustle. Owners who compete recognize this.

## Brand voice

- Direct. Confident. Rider-to-rider.
- Short sentences. No medical jargon unless we immediately translate it.
- No emojis in captions unless the tone of a specific post calls for it.
- Never fluffy, never cutesy. We're professionals who happen to wear boots.
- When in doubt: would a ropers' wife who trains barrels screenshot this and
  send it to her husband? If yes, ship it. If not, rewrite.

## Booking / CTA patterns (HARD RULE)

Every post's CTA must push to ONE of these two actions — nothing else:

1. **DM to book** — "DM 'BOOK' to grab a spot," "DM us to book,"
   "Send us a DM to lock in a time," etc.
2. **Book Now** — "Book now — link in bio," "Tap the link in bio to book,"
   "Book now at shortgochiro.com," "Book now — (406) 799-3369."

Pick whichever fits the post. Rotate phrasing, but never end a post with
"learn more," "check us out," "link in bio" (without "book"), or any
call-to-action that doesn't move the viewer toward booking. The point of
every post is booked appointments.

Location/date context is allowed and encouraged inside the CTA:
- "DM 'BOOK' — we're in Coppell Thursday."
- "Book now — we're running DFW this weekend."
- "Heading to Great Falls next week. DM to book."
